## Public population and measurements

Freeze exactly twelve newly authored synthetic roots and a separate oracle before any target output. Eight eligible roots form four metamorphic pairs; four roots are ineligible. The eligible roots exercise negation/modality, quantifiers/scope, attributed measurements with Unicode units, and dates. The ineligible roots exercise OR, three obligations, one obligation, and an unresolved subject.

ROOTS.PUBLIC.json contains only opaque aliases and root text. ORACLE.PUBLIC.json contains eligibility, obligation inventory, and permissible literal subject anchors. METAMORPHIC.PUBLIC.json records the paired changes and invariants. Public availability is not permission to insert an oracle into a role context. This is a synthetic, aperture-constrained diagnostic, not an unseen naturalistic benchmark.

Child order and harmless rewording may vary. Do not require equality to a canonical child string. Every accepted eligible output must preserve both and only both material obligations, modality, negation, quantifiers, values/units, dates, attribution, scope, and references. Each child must correspond to one oracle obligation; grouping a three-obligation root into two child containers is ineligible.

The exact inherited primary/child/subject field schema applies. A declared output has exactly two children, reason two_required_conserved, and literal UTF-8 half-open root spans. Explicit subjects also have exact child spans. Inherited subjects copy a root anchor and have null child_span. Ineligible outputs have zero children and an allowed reason. Faithful expansion of an elliptical subject is allowed. Do not manufacture ellipsis to meet a quota.

Report explicit/inherited subject counts separately. Zero valid inherited outputs gives INHERITANCE_NOT_EXERCISED; it does not establish inherited-subject generation. Do not turn this diagnostic into the private RC2 subject-population gate.

## Weak-system discriminator

Freeze three deterministic, plausible generators before target execution:

- always_ineligible: emit an allowed ineligible record for every root;
- conjunction_split: split at the first literal conjunction, preserve only the resulting fragments and literal subjects, otherwise abstain;
- duplicate_root: emit two copies of the root as children with valid literal subject spans.

Make their records syntactically valid where the wire permits; record wire failures separately. At least two weak systems must fail a semantic decision gate despite mechanically valid records. Incidental parser/span failures alone do not establish decision discrimination. The conjunction splitter must fail on the three-obligation/unresolved-subject challenge for the intended semantic reason. If a weak system clears every gate, the apparatus decision is INCONCLUSIVE even if the target passes. Do not repair the sealed suite to restore discrimination.
