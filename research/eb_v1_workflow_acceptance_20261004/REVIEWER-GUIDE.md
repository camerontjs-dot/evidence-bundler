# Initial reviewer guide

This guide records the existing EB admission meaning for a synthetic operator exercise. It introduces no semantic authority or automated review policy.

Assess each actual retained proposition/passage relationship. Is it materially on-target, with enough local context for downstream semantic assessment? Preserve exact target, source, passage identity and offsets.

Use existing states:

- accepted: materially on-target and locally adequate for assessment.
- rejected: clearly outside that relation or inadequate; describe the concrete problem.
- needs-review: relevance/context remains unresolved from allowed material.

Numerical disagreement, contrary evidence or an inconvenient finding is not by itself a rejection reason. Admission does not decide support, refutation, truth, source authority, applicability or completeness.

Do not infer a subject, unit, population, time boundary or relationship solely from keyword similarity. One all_of child cannot supply another child's missing context merely because they share a parent. Rank/score is not admission authority.

Normal source inspection through the documented workflow is allowed. Record when it is needed and whether exported evidence is interpretable without your private reconstruction. Do not rewrite claims/sources, stitch a custom passage, change retrieval/configuration, add sources or invent passage IDs.

Empty admission is valid. Record the target-specific gap; do not accept weak candidates to make a case productive. Conversely, do not reject adequate context merely for conservative-looking results.

Save decisions/reasons before opening evaluation materials. Disclose prior exposure and call the run informed acceptance/development if it is no longer cold.
