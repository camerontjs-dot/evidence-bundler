## Failure / stop states

the one fresh writer attempt completes but does not return a complete required profile surface.

BLOCKED_CUSTODY_VERIFICATION:
the invocation/response/receipt/native-custody/check route cannot complete successfully after the writer attempt begins.

BLOCKED_AUTHORITY:
required pinned GitHub or runtime identity cannot be established before invocation.

CONTAMINATED:
forbidden material is exposed before profile freeze.

INCONCLUSIVE:
the apparatus executes but the frozen checks cannot distinguish a valid prerequisite setup from an invalid one.

SUPPORTED_FOR_GENERATION_EXECUTION:
all acceptance conditions above are observed.
